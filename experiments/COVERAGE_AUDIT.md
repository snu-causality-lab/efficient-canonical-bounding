## Additional Experiment Provenance

The directories `random_admg_sweep/`, `scalability_demo/`,
`lazy_phase1/`, `prop42_tightness/`, and `balke_pearl_iv/` are retained
for provenance. Their compact CSVs document additional validation experiments, but
the final paper table numbers are governed by the stable shards listed above.

## Known Scope Limitations

- `build_signature_quotient` explicitly enumerates the lifted canonical domain.
  Very large lifted domains require structural/lazy routines rather than the
  explicit builder.
- Scheduler scripts are templates. Public users should set `PYTHON_BIN` and
  `REPO_ROOT` as needed, and add cluster-specific output paths or node
  constraints before running them.
- The repository includes an MIT license at the project root.
